## Full description (max 4000)
Experience extreme motorcycle racing. Master the roads, dodge traffic and become the ultimate rider! GOT RIDER is an endless 3D traffic racer built for quick, addictive runs. Weave through busy highway traffic, pull off daring near misses, hit boost and see how far you can go. RULE THE ROAD.

TILT TO STEER
Hold your phone like a handlebar and tilt to change lanes. No buttons needed. Tap to boost, swipe down to brake.

SPEED
Normal driving reaches up to 160 km/h and boost pushes the fastest machines past 300 km/h.

17 RIDES TO UNLOCK
Start with a City Cycle and earn credits to unlock motorbikes, an adventure bike, sedans, a rally car, a pickup, a muscle car, an electric hypercar and more. Every vehicle has its own speed, handling and armor.

ENDLESS LEVELS & RANKS
Keep levelling up from Rookie to Rider Legend and beyond. Every level-up pays a credit bonus.

MISSIONS THAT NEVER END
Always 3 active missions: near misses, distance, top speed, score, wrong-way driving, coins and more. Finish one and a harder one appears.

IMPOSSIBLE CHALLENGES
Dare to try a nearly impossible one-run challenge? A voice will mock you while you drive. Beat it for a huge reward, then face an even harder one. Voice can be turned off in settings.

NEW TRAFFIC & OBSTACLES
Police cars with sirens, ambulances and auto-rickshaws share the road. Pick Classic for pure traffic, or Hazard mode for road cones and work barriers with x1.25 rewards.

REAL SOUND
Per-vehicle engine sounds that rise with your speed, tyre screech, crashes, crowd reactions and music. Every sound can be switched on or off in settings.

WRONG-WAY BONUS
The left half of the road is oncoming traffic. Drive there for x2 score, if you dare!

DAY, EVENING & NIGHT
Watch the sky change from bright day to glowing sunset to dark night.

TWO WAYS TO RIDE
- Bikes and cycles: portrait, tilt steering
- Cars: landscape with GAS, BRAKE, REVERSE and BOOST pads

FREE, OFFLINE, NO ADS
No internet needed. No ads. No account. Progress is saved on your device.
